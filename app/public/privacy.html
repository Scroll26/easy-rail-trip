Rail Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung unten

Rail Planner (scroll26.github.io/rail-planner) is a free, open-source trip planner. It is a website that runs in your browser. It has no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. A small helper service run by the developer (a Cloudflare Worker) is used only if you turn on Google Drive sync or share your trip with a live link; both are described below.

What is stored, and where

  • Everything you enter (places, people, timeline, groups, settings) is stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage).
  • Deleting the app or clearing your browser data deletes this information from that device.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose Settings → Sync → Connect Google Drive:

  • You sign in with Google, and the app asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This lets the app create, read and update its own file in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any of your other files.
  • Your trip is copied into that file so your devices can share it. The transfer goes directly between your browser and Google. Your trip does not pass through any server of the developer.
  • So that you stay signed in, the sign-in goes through the helper: it trades Google's one-time sign-in code for a lasting access key, encrypts it with a key only the helper knows, and gives it back to your browser, which keeps it in local storage. Later the helper uses it to get new one-hour access tokens for your browser. The helper stores nothing and never sees your trip.
  • Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide this sync feature. It is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Rail Planner's use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can stop syncing at any time with Settings → Sync → Disconnect, and remove the app's access in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access there also lets you delete the app's hidden data from your Drive (Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Rail Planner → Delete hidden app data).

Optional sharing of your trip

If you share your trip (Trip → Share), people with the link can see it read-only:

  • Shared are your stops, connections, things to do and map settings. Not shared: prices and your budget, places that aren't part of the trip, and people's addresses and contact details (only their name and roughly where they live).
  • Live link: a copy of these shared parts is stored by the helper (Cloudflare Workers KV) and updated when you change your trip, until you choose Stop sharing, which deletes it. Anyone who has the link can see it.
  • Snapshot link: the shared parts are packed into the link itself and not stored anywhere by the app. Anyone who has the link can see them, and it can't be switched off.

Other services the app contacts

To work, your browser loads data from these services directly. They receive your IP address and the request, as with any website:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the app itself
  • OpenFreeMap for map tiles (the map area you look at)
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap, only when you search or add a place (the search text or map position)
  • OpenRailwayMap for the railway lines (the map area you look at)
  • Transitous, only when you look up direct trains (the chosen station and time)
  • open.er-api.com, falling back to jsDelivr, for exchange rates when the currency overlay is on (no personal data)
  • Google, only if you use Drive sync (see above)
  • Cloudflare, which runs the helper, only if you use Drive sync or open or make a live link

The app sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Scroll26/rail-planner/issues.

Datenschutzerklärung (Deutsch)

Rail Planner ist ein kostenloser Reiseplaner, der in deinem Browser läuft. Es gibt keine Benutzerkonten, keine Analyse und keine Werbung. Ein kleiner Hilfsdienst des Entwicklers (ein Cloudflare Worker) wird nur für die Google-Drive-Synchronisierung und für Live-Links zum Teilen genutzt.

  • Alles, was du eingibst, wird nur auf deinem Gerät im Browser gespeichert.
  • Google-Drive-Synchronisierung (freiwillig): Die App fragt nur die Berechtigung drive.appdata an. Damit kann sie ausschließlich ihre eigene Datei in einem versteckten App-Ordner deines Google Drive anlegen, lesen und ändern, aber keine anderen Dateien sehen. Die Übertragung läuft direkt zwischen deinem Browser und Google. Daten aus Google-APIs werden nur für die Synchronisierung genutzt, nicht weitergegeben, nicht für Werbung und nicht zum Training von KI-Modellen verwendet (gemäß der Google API Services User Data Policy, inkl. „Limited Use“). Damit du angemeldet bleibst, tauscht der Hilfsdienst den Anmeldecode gegen einen dauerhaften Zugangsschlüssel, verschlüsselt ihn mit einem nur ihm bekannten Schlüssel und gibt ihn an deinen Browser zurück. Er speichert nichts und sieht deine Reise nie.
  • Teilen (freiwillig): Geteilt werden Stopps, Verbindungen, Unternehmungen und Karteneinstellungen, nicht aber Preise, Budget, Orte außerhalb der Reise sowie Adressen und Kontaktdaten von Personen. Beim Live-Link speichert der Hilfsdienst (Cloudflare Workers KV) eine Kopie davon, bis du „Stop sharing“ wählst. Beim Snapshot-Link steckt alles im Link selbst und wird nirgends gespeichert. Wer den Link hat, kann die Reise ansehen.
  • Zugriff entziehen: Einstellungen → Sync → Disconnect in der App oder unter myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Für Karte, Suche und Wechselkurse lädt dein Browser Daten direkt von GitHub Pages, OpenFreeMap, Nominatim/OpenStreetMap und open.er-api.com bzw. jsDelivr (bei Sync und Live-Links auch von Cloudflare). Diese Dienste sehen dabei deine IP-Adresse. Die App setzt keine eigenen Cookies und verwendet kein Tracking.
  • Fragen: github.com/Scroll26/rail-planner/issues
