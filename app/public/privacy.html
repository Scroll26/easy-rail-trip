Rail Planner – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Deutsche Fassung unten

Rail Planner (scroll26.github.io/rail-planner) is a free, open-source trip planner. It is a website that runs entirely in your browser. It has no server, no user accounts, no analytics and no advertising. The developer never receives your data.

What is stored, and where

  • Everything you enter (places, people, timeline, groups, settings) is stored only on your device, in your browser's local storage (IndexedDB / localStorage).
  • Deleting the app or clearing your browser data deletes this information from that device.

Optional Google Drive sync

If you choose Settings → Sync → Connect Google Drive:

  • You sign in with Google, and the app asks for one permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata. This lets the app create, read and update its own file in a hidden app folder of your Google Drive. It cannot see, read or change any of your other files.
  • Your trip is copied into that file so your devices can share it. The transfer goes directly between your browser and Google. It does not pass through any server of the developer.
  • The temporary sign-in token is kept in your browser's local storage on that device and expires after about one hour.
  • Data received from Google APIs is used only to provide this sync feature. It is not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Rail Planner's use of Google user data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can stop syncing at any time with Settings → Sync → Disconnect, and remove the app's access in your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing access there also lets you delete the app's hidden data from your Drive (Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Rail Planner → Delete hidden app data).

Other services the app contacts

To work, your browser loads data from these services directly. They receive your IP address and the request, as with any website:

  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the app itself
  • OpenFreeMap for map tiles (the map area you look at)
  • Nominatim / OpenStreetMap, only when you search or add a place (the search text or map position)
  • OpenRailwayMap for the railway lines (the map area you look at)
  • Transitous, only when you look up direct trains (the chosen station and time)
  • open.er-api.com, falling back to jsDelivr, for exchange rates when the currency overlay is on (no personal data)
  • Google, only if you use Drive sync (see above)

The app sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/Scroll26/rail-planner/issues.

Datenschutzerklärung (Deutsch)

Rail Planner ist ein kostenloser Reiseplaner, der vollständig in deinem Browser läuft. Es gibt keinen Server, keine Benutzerkonten, keine Analyse und keine Werbung. Der Entwickler erhält keine deiner Daten.

  • Alles, was du eingibst, wird nur auf deinem Gerät im Browser gespeichert.
  • Google-Drive-Synchronisierung (freiwillig): Die App fragt nur die Berechtigung drive.appdata an. Damit kann sie ausschließlich ihre eigene Datei in einem versteckten App-Ordner deines Google Drive anlegen, lesen und ändern, aber keine anderen Dateien sehen. Die Übertragung läuft direkt zwischen deinem Browser und Google. Daten aus Google-APIs werden nur für die Synchronisierung genutzt, nicht weitergegeben, nicht für Werbung und nicht zum Training von KI-Modellen verwendet (gemäß der Google API Services User Data Policy, inkl. „Limited Use“).
  • Zugriff entziehen: Einstellungen → Sync → Disconnect in der App oder unter myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Für Karte, Suche und Wechselkurse lädt dein Browser Daten direkt von GitHub Pages, OpenFreeMap, Nominatim/OpenStreetMap und open.er-api.com bzw. jsDelivr. Diese Dienste sehen dabei deine IP-Adresse. Die App setzt keine eigenen Cookies und verwendet kein Tracking.
  • Fragen: github.com/Scroll26/rail-planner/issues
